Memoir · Flash Fiction · Journalism · Research

Stories that live between two languages

From a memoir about losing my words at a family celebration, to a feature on the students I've tutored in Huế, writing is how I make sense of the in-between. Here you'll find my creative work, my reporting for The Governor's Review — the journal I co-founded — and my John Locke Institute essay.

Leadership & Service.

02

Tutors for a Cause · AAFC · Special Olympics

Passing on what education gave me

What began in eighth grade as one volunteer on a shaky video call is now a team of 15 tutors serving 200+ students in Vietnam, which I lead as Co-President of Tutors for a Cause. I also organized the regional Asian American Footsteps Conference for 200+ students from 20+ schools and direct our school's Special Olympics event — each a way of turning what I've been given into something I can pass on.

Performing Arts.

03

Dance · Theater · Singing

Three ways to tell a story without writing it down

After ten years of dance training I choreograph my own contemporary and lyrical pieces, I've played Amy in Little Women and Lisa in Mamma Mia!, and I sing with our Advanced Chorus and a cappella group. Each has taught me something different — trust in the people moving beside me, presence under the lights, and how to listen harder than I sing.

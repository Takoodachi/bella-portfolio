Performing Arts

Every stage I can find

Dance, theater, and singing — three different ways of telling a story without writing it down.

Dance Theater Singing

Category One

Dance

Dance is where I stop translating. Choreography asks for honesty the way writing does — a phrase, a breath, a held moment — but the language is entirely physical. I perform with my school's dance company in contemporary and ensemble pieces, and every show teaches me something new about trusting the people moving beside me.

Category Two

Theater

On stage, I get to live inside someone else's story for a while — and bring the audience with me. From ensemble numbers to featured roles in our school productions, theater has taught me projection, presence, and how much work hides behind a single effortless-looking scene.

Category Three

Singing

In choir, no voice works alone. Singing has taught me the discipline of blend — listening harder than you sing, tuning to the people around you, and carrying your own line anyway. From holiday concerts to spring showcases, it's the closest thing I know to the "beautiful song" I once felt outside of; here, I know the lyrics.
